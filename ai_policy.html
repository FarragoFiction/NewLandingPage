JR's Zampanio Branch Ai Policy

Hi yes hello, it's me, JR, your friendly neighborhood spiral faced horror/waste.

I wanted to be clear about how I use generative AI, and more importantly, how I don't. (and unfortunately for us all when I decide I want to be clear I get hella wordy, lol)

I find LLM (large language models, another word for generative AI) to be unsettling, or even dangerous! "AI Psychosis" scares the shit out of me!

I also find the WAY LLMs are currently being used (to replace human workers, to make even more profit for billionaires, to make it easier and easier for people to stop talking to each other and learn new skills) high key terrifying as well!

So I do what I always do with my fears: I make art about it. Art I try to keep as ethical as possible, with as much transparency as possible. I make horror content about obession, about losing yourself to something so utterly you can't even recognize your past self. I'm no stranger to the legitimate dangers this can cause just as a topic to explore. I try to have content warnings, to give people guardrails, to learn about dark patterns so I can specifical disrupt them. So this rambling 'policy' is kind of me trying to do the same thing for AI use.

My goal with having a small amount of LLM content in my branch of Zampanio is to play with the ideas it causes in me. IS there a safe way to engage with something like this? The early LLM models, like Deep Dream, were so fascinatingly liminal, like half remembered dreams. Can I, as a human, make content that invokes that, using LLM images as a tone setter? I want to use my art as a way to familiarize myself with something I find scary. What can LLM actually do, and how worried do I have to be about its place in my future?

If I use LLM generated content (or take LLM content donated from a fan) I try to make it as clearly marked as possible. Either old type really bad and uncanny images or literally screenshot it on the chatgpt page or whatever. They tend to be either on eyedolgames.com or if you get deep enough into the bathroom that it starts breaking down (if you know you know).

I code and write myself. I make dioramas these days, also by myself. No AI touchups, often even no spellchecker (I embrace the typos, lol). I collaborate with writers, artists and musicians who, to my knowledge, arnen't using LLM. I also collaborate with specific LLM enthusiest I know are using LLM as ethically as anyone can these days.

I add LLM content deliberately in places where I feel it works, not as some kind of normal part of my creation process. Usually weird obviously AI images in infinite fake websites. Once I trained an AI on my own voice and put that in the bathroom as "Truth"... Past me making hundreds of audio logs... yeah. My voice is gonna be stolen by a scammer. I wanted to explore what that would feel like before it really happened, you know?

I'm not aware of any ai generated music in my branch of Zampanio. I think theres an LLM podcast about Zampanio somewhere? Not anywhere you find in a normal playthrough, somewhere I just hid for myself to remember how it felt to hear these manic people talking and hallucinating about something I'm deeply familiar with... I wanted to undrestand how to detect it when it WASN'T something I knew about? If that makes any sense.

Any AI generated images are intended to be as obviously bad and fake as possible. I know actual artists irl, they're not gonna let me get away with (if i even wanted to) passing off ai slop as their work, you know?

I'm not aware of any ai generated writing in my branch of zampanio, barring times where I am deliberately asking an AI something about zampanio and take screenshots of its response or otherwise label it as an interview with an AI. (all those are in eyedolgames.com, pretty sure.)

I use local models running on my own computer and not some datacenter (as does my main human contributors who use LLM) rather than stupid corperate ones that are bad for just about everyone. I specifically use local LLMs to generate uncanny, unsettling, images that I feel like there's no real analogue from human created? The point of eyedolgames.com is that its run by inhuman monsters absolutely sure they are aboslutely nailing blending in as human. They create eerie dream like infinite sites they think appeals to humans, of which they absolutely are.
